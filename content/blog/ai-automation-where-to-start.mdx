## The three leaks we look for first

1. **Missed calls and slow follow-up.** Every call that goes to voicemail is a customer
   deciding whether to try the next business on the list.
2. **Manual admin.** Quotes, invoices, reminders and CRM updates that someone re-types
   by hand every week.
3. **Disconnected tools.** Five to fifteen SaaS products that were bought one at a time
   and never talk to each other.
